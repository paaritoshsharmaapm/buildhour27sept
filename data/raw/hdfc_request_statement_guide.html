    Request Statement

    We offer you the convenience of having your account statement sent to your email id registered with us. Please enter your folio number in the box provided below and choose the option in which you wish to receive your account statement.

    Pursuant to SEBI guidelines, with effect from October 1, 2011, an allotment confirmation specifying the units allotted shall be sent by way of email and/or SMS within 5 Business Days of the initial investment transaction to the Unit holders who have registered their e-mail address and/or mobile number. 

    Thereafter, a Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) shall be sent to the Unit holder in whose folio transactions have taken place during that month, on or before 10th of the succeeding month by mail/ email as applicable. 

    In case of specific request received from investors, Mutual Fund will provide an account statement to the investors within 5 Business Days from the receipt of such request.
